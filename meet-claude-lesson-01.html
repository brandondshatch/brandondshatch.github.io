Door 01

Chat.

Your thinking partner.

A conversation window. Rubber-duck a client problem. Draft an email. Edit a paragraph. Brainstorm a new service line.

— The smartest coworker you can message at eleven at night.

Chat · Try this now

A recurring email, for free.

Draft a warm monthly check-in I can send any client — friendly, four sentences, no corporate tone.

In thirty seconds, you have a template you can reuse every month.
Door 02

Cowork.

Claude inside your files.

You point Claude at a folder on your Mac. It reads your documents, drafts new ones, and organizes what's already there — in place.

— An operations assistant who can see your desk.

Cowork · Try this now

Turn a folder into an SOP.

Read this client folder and draft a one-page SOP my team can follow for new-client onboarding.

In five minutes, a first-pass SOP pulled from your real work.
