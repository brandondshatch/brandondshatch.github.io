About

With 30+ years in video production and creative storytelling, Marin Production Group has evolved from traditional documentary filmmaking to cutting-edge AI-accelerated workflows and generative video production.

Notable work includes the Fountainhead Antique Auto Museum documentaries, content for PLMI (Personalized Lifestyle Medicine Institute), GrowthX AI content strategy, and the viral BackBone Pro video that garnered 2.8 million views.

Our current focus is helping brands adapt to the modern content landscape through AI-enhanced production workflows that combine decades of storytelling expertise with the latest technological innovations.

Based in Marin County, California, our expertise spans video editing, documentary filmmaking, content strategy, copywriting, marketing, and AI-powered creative workflows.
