what i do

I shape complex scripted and unscripted material into clear, emotionally resonant stories.

My work lives at the intersection of documentary, branded content, and emerging AI-assisted production—where interviews are authentic, emotion is paramount, and meaning is discovered and not imposed.

I'm an editor that thinks like a storyteller, a director enthralled with my interviewees, and a systems designer that automates mundanity and spotlights humanity.

how i work

I start with meaning, not mechanics

I look for arc, contrast, implication, and subtext before I touch pacing, music, or polish. My first pass is about what the piece is really saying.

I'm especially strong at:

  • Story & concept
  • Finding narrative shape in long interviews
  • Re-ordering quotes without distorting truth
  • Clarifying intent and discovering heart

Reality-style, multi-camera, vlogs, field recordings, archival, and generative—all in service of story.

I routinely handle:

  • Lengthy source interviews
  • Proxy workflows, cloud collaboration, and creative pivots
  • Heavy revision without compromising narrative integrity

I design processes workflows using:

  • Transcription-driven editing
  • AI-assisted analysis for structure, fact-checking, and story refinement
  • Clear handoffs between shooters, editors, and stakeholders

Where AI Fits (and Where It Doesn't)

I use AI as an accelerant not as a crutch.
