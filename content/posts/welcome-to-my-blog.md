I've been thinking about starting a blog for a while now. As someone who works at the intersection of **video editing**, **storytelling**, and **AI-assisted production**, I wanted a space to share what I'm learning and explore ideas more deeply.

## Why Now?

The creative landscape is changing faster than ever. AI tools are transforming how we work, but the fundamentals of storytelling remain constant. I want to document this journey—the tools that work, the workflows that don't, and the questions that keep me up at night.

### What You'll Find Here

This blog will cover:

* **Video editing techniques** and post-production workflows
* **Storytelling frameworks** for documentary and branded content
* **AI-assisted production** tips and experiments
* Behind-the-scenes looks at projects
* Lessons learned from mistakes (there are many)

## Story First, Always

Here's what I believe: No matter how good the tools get, they're only as valuable as the story they help you tell. Technology should *amplify* human creativity, not replace it.

> "The best edit is invisible. The best story feels inevitable." 

That's the standard I hold myself to, and it's what I'll explore here.
