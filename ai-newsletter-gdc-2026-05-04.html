Convert to Tasks

Meetings generate the to-dos that drive client work. The opportunity is not to automate judgment, but to turn clear action items into structured tasks with a reliable review step. Modern tools now make that pipeline much easier to build.

Framework lens: Volume • Leverage • AI Fit • Usability • Ease

Operational Notes

Fathom AI

Fathom's platform update adds per-meeting capture choices: bot-free transcription, bot-free audio capture, or full video capture. Live summaries can appear during the call, scratchpad notes can merge into the final summary, and Ask Fathom can search across a user's meeting library with citations to exact transcript moments.

Read AI

Read AI introduced Ada for scheduling, knowledge-base questions, and follow-ups. It also added botless Google Meet capture through the Meet Media API, plus Notion, Confluence, Slack, ChatGPT, Claude, Chrome extension, and mobile app paths for moving meeting context where work already happens.

Make

Make AI Agents now live directly inside the visual scenario builder. They can interpret inputs, choose tools, adapt inside workflows, expose reasoning, accept file inputs such as PDFs and CSVs, and be refined through chat inside the canvas.

Ramp and QuickBooks

Ramp added AI token-spend reporting by provider, model, API key, and team, plus CLI and MCP integrations for finance workflows. QuickBooks Online added payroll garnishment tracking, larger Bill Pay batches, and natural-language insights through Intuit Intelligence.

Example Workflow

  1. Capture: Use bot-free Fathom or botless Read AI capture for an onboarding call, selecting audio-only when client sensitivity calls for it.
  2. Summarize and extract: Ask for action items, document requests, deadlines, and follow-up questions immediately after the call.
  3. Convert to tasks: Use a Make agent to parse the action items and create ClickUp tasks with due dates, notes, and context.
  4. Review and refine: Have a coordinator check the newly created tasks, adjust priorities, and improve the prompt or agent settings over time.

How to Apply It

  • Start with internal meetings before using the pipeline on client conversations.
  • Choose capture modes case by case, especially when clients are sensitive about recording.
  • Pass only the necessary information into connected systems.
  • Keep final decisions about scope, priority, and communication with the team.

What This Does Not Mean

This does not replace judgment-heavy work, tax decisions, client understanding, or relationship nuance. It also does not mean entire recordings should be sent into generative models indiscriminately. The sweet spot is clearly stated action items with a human review step.

Practical starting point: Pilot a meeting-to-task pipeline on one recurring internal call, measure whether it saves review time, then decide whether the workflow earns a client-facing version.
